WHEELCHAIR-FIRST TECHNOLOGY

Stay seated.
Keep moving.

Meet LegMaker — a developing lower-limb cycling platform designed to attach directly to a compatible manual wheelchair. Motion begins where the user already sits.

Built around the wheelchair.
Not the other way around.

LegMaker is designed to connect to a compatible manual wheelchair so the user does not need to transfer to a separate exercise seat. Compared with independently positioned floor-standing cycling equipment, its defining design direction is direct frame integration.
