THE FRAMEWORK

Questions worth answering.

Every proposed evaluation starts with measurable questions and documented safeguards—not assumed results.

01 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Wheelchair compatibility

Assess frame dimensions, mounting stability, seating and accessibility before any use.

02 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Mechanical hazards

Evaluate pinch points, moving parts, restraint interactions and entrapment risks.

03 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Electrical & battery

Verify charging, enclosure integrity, thermal behavior and electrical protection.

04 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Emergency stop

Test stop accessibility, response behavior and recovery after faults.

05 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Human factors

Document setup, instructions, supervision, operator training and stopping criteria.

06 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Incident handling

Establish adverse-event, device-deficiency and corrective-action procedures before studies.
