LegMaker / MMA research concept

Adapt the training environment, not the ambition.

A wheelchair-first perspective on the mma challenge—and a research direction worth exploring.

Understand the challenge ↓
01 / The human challenge

Why this matters.

Athletes in adaptive combat sports may struggle to find accessible options for supplementary lower-limb movement work, particularly when equipment requires transfers.

02 / The investigational idea

What if movement could stay with the wheelchair?

Explore a structured, supervised seated cycling research concept that can be investigated independently of sparring or combat training.

LegMaker is an investigational wheelchair-mounted lower-limb exercise concept. Suitability depends on the individual, device compatibility, supervision and future evaluation.

03 / The research pathway

What would we need to prove?

Measure usability, session repeatability, participant comfort and safety. No claim of improved fight performance is made.

Understand

Interview users and practitioners to define the real workflow and unmet needs.

Evaluate

Test usability, safety requirements and practical feasibility under an appropriate protocol.

Validate

Report measured results transparently before making performance or health claims.

Research concept only. This page does not describe an operating mma laboratory or establish clinical effectiveness, regulatory clearance or validated sport- or military-specific outcomes. Photography illustrates the existing prototype, not a completed mma deployment.

THE BARRIER

What makes access difficult?

Accessible exercise outside technical training may be difficult when conventional machines require transfers.

Four steps in a possible evaluation

  1. 01 — Define the settingAthlete and qualified supervisor separate the exercise concept from sparring or combat.
  2. 02 — Check safety and fitDevice compatibility, fit and stopping procedures are reviewed.
  3. 03 — Explore a supervised sessionA controlled session examines practical operation and comfort.
  4. 04 — Review the evidenceResearchers evaluate usability; no combat-sport performance improvement is claimed.
What exists today: an investigational wheelchair-mounted exercise prototype and a proposed mma research concept. What does not yet exist: a verified mma lab service, published lab-specific outcomes, or a guarantee of clinical or athletic benefit.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a working MMA training or clinical service?

No. This page describes a proposed research direction. Availability, eligibility and any future study depend on appropriate partners and approvals.

Can anyone use LegMaker immediately?

No. Wheelchair compatibility, individual safety considerations, supervision and investigational status must be assessed. Do not treat this page as medical advice or operating instructions.
