WHAT WE WOULD MEASURE

Metrics that matter.

RPM · workload · setup time · wheelchair fit · session completion · safety events · emergency-stop response · battery runtime.

EVIDENCE ROADMAP

Prove before promising.

  1. Bench testing and risk analysis
  2. Study design and regulatory assessment
  3. IRB / FDA review when applicable
  4. Approved feasibility work
  5. Transparent reporting of results

Research status: These are proposed investigational concepts. This page does not announce IRB approval, FDA authorization, active recruitment, verified outcomes or clinical availability. Free introductory consultations are not enrollment in a clinical study.
