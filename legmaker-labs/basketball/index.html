LegMaker / Basketball research concept

The athlete is more than the game clock.

A wheelchair-first perspective on the basketball challenge—and a research direction worth exploring.

Understand the challenge ↓
01 / The human challenge

Why this matters.

A wheelchair basketball player may need structured conditioning or rehabilitation outside court sessions. Traditional equipment may be inconvenient when it requires leaving the chair or accessing a separate station.

02 / The investigational idea

What if movement could stay with the wheelchair?

Explore the feasibility of lower-limb cycling research while the participant remains in a compatible wheelchair.

LegMaker is an investigational wheelchair-mounted lower-limb exercise concept. Suitability depends on the individual, device compatibility, supervision and future evaluation.

03 / The research pathway

What would we need to prove?

Investigate session setup, cadence measurement, comfort, duration and user-reported experience. No claim of better basketball performance is made.

Understand

Interview users and practitioners to define the real workflow and unmet needs.

Evaluate

Test usability, safety requirements and practical feasibility under an appropriate protocol.

Validate

Report measured results transparently before making performance or health claims.

Research concept only. This page does not describe an operating basketball laboratory or establish clinical effectiveness, regulatory clearance or validated sport- or military-specific outcomes. Photography illustrates the existing prototype, not a completed basketball deployment.
