01 / The human challenge

Why this matters.

Clinical teams coordinate patient movement, equipment, staffing and therapy schedules. Transferring to a separate exercise station may introduce additional workflow demands for some patients.

02 / The investigational idea

What if movement could stay with the wheelchair?

Investigate whether wheelchair-integrated lower-limb cycling could be evaluated within a supervised rehabilitation workflow.

LegMaker is an investigational wheelchair-mounted lower-limb exercise concept. Suitability depends on the individual, device compatibility, supervision and future evaluation.

03 / The research pathway

What would we need to prove?

Define clinical eligibility, infection control, safety protocols, device compatibility, staffing needs and measurable endpoints before any clinical efficacy claim.

Understand

Interview users and practitioners to define the real workflow and unmet needs.

Evaluate

Test usability, safety requirements and practical feasibility under an appropriate protocol.

Validate

Report measured results transparently before making performance or health claims.

Research concept only. This page does not describe an operating hospital laboratory or establish clinical effectiveness, regulatory clearance or validated sport- or military-specific outcomes. Photography illustrates the existing prototype, not a completed hospital deployment.

THE BARRIER

What makes access difficult?

Patient transfers, staffing, cleaning and scheduling can complicate introduction of exercise equipment.

Four steps in a possible evaluation

  1. 01 — Define the settingClinicians define eligibility, research endpoints and institutional approvals.
  2. 02 — Check safety and fitStaff assess infection-control requirements, compatibility and safety procedures.
  3. 03 — Explore a supervised sessionA supervised protocol evaluates workflow and patient tolerance when appropriate.
  4. 04 — Review the evidenceThe team documents feasibility and adverse events under an approved study design; no treatment benefit is assumed.
What exists today: an investigational wheelchair-mounted exercise prototype and a proposed hospital research concept. What does not yet exist: a verified hospital lab service, published lab-specific outcomes, or a guarantee of clinical or athletic benefit.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a working Hospital training or clinical service?

No. This page describes a proposed research direction. Availability, eligibility and any future study depend on appropriate partners and approvals.

Can anyone use LegMaker immediately?

No. Wheelchair compatibility, individual safety considerations, supervision and investigational status must be assessed. Do not treat this page as medical advice or operating instructions.

What can a research partner do next?

Contact Rehabwheel to discuss the problem, feasibility questions, possible study design and requirements for a responsible evaluation.
