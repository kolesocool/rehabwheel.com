THE FRAMEWORK

Questions worth answering.

Every proposed evaluation starts with measurable questions and documented safeguards—not assumed results.

01 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Feasibility

Can wheelchair users and operators set up and use the prototype reliably under supervision?

02 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Engineering

Are speed, resistance, battery and emergency-stop functions repeatable and verifiable?

03 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Usability

What does setup time, comfort, operator workload and session completion reveal?

04 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Measurement

How reliably can device metrics such as RPM, revolutions and workload be captured?

05 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Study design

Define populations, eligibility, comparator, endpoints and statistical methods before any claims.

06 / RESEARCH PRIORITY

Ethics

Appropriate independent review, informed consent, data protection and regulatory assessment precede research.
