GOOD TO KNOW

What you should know before launch.

Can I record my own sports sessions?

Not publicly yet. Activity journaling is a planned feature. Joining the waitlist does not create a personal activity account.

Are clubs and events real and accepting members?

No. We are not offering live Community clubs or event registration at this stage.

Can I connect LegMaker or share clinical records?

No. Community is a separate planned social product, and the waitlist is not a medical intake or device-data portal. Do not submit health records.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can join the mobile-friendly waitlist on your phone. The social app is not launched, and no App Store or Google Play release date is confirmed.
