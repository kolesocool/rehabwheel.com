LEGAL · CONFIDENTIALITY

Proprietary Information & Confidentiality Policy

Effective Date: October 2026
Entity: Rehabwheel Inc.

1. Statement of Confidentiality

Rehabwheel Inc. ("Rehabwheel," "we," "our," or "us") develops advanced physical artificial intelligence, medical devices, and assistive mobility hardware, including our foundational LegMaker technology. Protecting our intellectual property, trade secrets, research data, and proprietary engineering designs is critical to our mission and competitive standing.

All technical documentation, source code, CAD models, circuit schematics, clinical trial protocols, financial projections, and strategic business plans associated with Rehabwheel Inc. constitute strict proprietary information.

2. Scope of Proprietary Assets

Our protected assets include, but are not limited to:

  • Engineering & R&D Data: Mechanical designs, custom microcontroller architectures, edge AI models, sensor network specifications, and manufacturing workflows developed across our San Francisco and Austin operations.
  • Clinical & Research Documentation: Proprietary clinical trial frameworks, physiological datasets, and collaborative research methodologies developed alongside academic partners such as Texas State University and UT Austin.
  • Commercial & Financial Records: Investor communications, capital strategies, pricing models, vendor agreements, and commercialization roadmaps (including engagements with the NVIDIA Inception ecosystem and Larta Institute).

3. Obligations of Visitors, Partners, and Personnel

  • Non-Disclosure: Unauthorized access, copying, reverse engineering, dissemination, or public disclosure of any proprietary materials found on this platform, in our catalogs, or shared during corporate interactions is strictly prohibited.
  • Authorized Access Only: Confidential documents, portal links, and technical specifications are provided exclusively to authorized stakeholders, verified clinical partners, and accredited institutional investors under strict non-disclosure covenants.
  • Reporting Breaches: Any suspected or actual unauthorized disclosure of Rehabwheel proprietary information must be reported immediately to our compliance team.

4. Legal Enforcement & Protection

Rehabwheel Inc. vigorously protects its intellectual property rights under United States federal and state laws, international trade secret protections, and patent statutes. Violations of confidentiality or unauthorized use of proprietary assets may result in legal action, including pursuit of available injunctive relief and damages as permitted by applicable law.
