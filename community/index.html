Good to know

A few things, made clear.

We're opening a waitlist, not a finished app. Here's what that means.

Is Rehabwheel Community available now?

Not yet. The public site is a waitlist for an upcoming community experience. The unfinished interactive preview is no longer offered publicly.

What is planned for Rehabwheel Community?

The product vision includes adaptive sports, personal activity tracking, inclusive clubs and events, and opportunities to connect. Features may change before launch.

Will I need a LegMaker device to participate?

No device is required to join the waitlist. Rehabwheel Community is a separate planned social product; LegMaker is an investigational medical-technology device.

Does joining the waitlist create an account?

No. Sending a request only shares your contact details for early-access updates. It does not create an account or guarantee an invitation or launch date.

What information should I enter?

An email address is required and your first name is optional. Please do not submit diagnoses, rehabilitation details, medical records, or other sensitive health information.

How can I unsubscribe?

Email nikita@rehabwheel.com with the subject “Unsubscribe Community Waitlist.” You can also request deletion of the contact details you submitted.
