Built around your wheelchair

LegMaker’s development focus is a lower-limb pedal unit that attaches to a manual wheelchair, bringing passive, active and resistance exercise into the user’s seated setup.

See the hardware in our prototype photographs, explore the LegMaker platform or contact our team about fit, development and evaluation opportunities.
