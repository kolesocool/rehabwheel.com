How to use this comparison.

Start by identifying the exact product model and the movement technology it provides. A prototype’s planned features cannot be treated as equivalent to a cleared device’s published indications. Ask each manufacturer for current documentation, setup requirements, support and availability.

Rehabwheel’s differentiating development focus is integrating a lower-limb pedal platform with the manual wheelchair. Read the LegMaker overview, examine the real prototype photographs and review the research status before forming a view about the product.

For individual exercise suitability and clinical decisions, discuss the device and its instructions with your care team. This comparison does not establish interchangeability or a treatment recommendation.
