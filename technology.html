SAFETY-BY-DESIGN · DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION

Safety belongs in the architecture.

01

Controlled motion

The product direction includes bounded, configurable motion parameters rather than an open-ended control model.

02

Session readiness

The interface concepts emphasize configuration and readiness before a movement session begins.

03

Device state

Connected concepts are designed to make device and session state visible to the user.

04

Development verification

Engineering verification and usability evaluation are part of the development approach.

These are design principles and development directions, not representations of certification, regulatory clearance, or validated safety performance.
