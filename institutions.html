Institutional buyer questions

Transparent answers for procurement, clinical and technical reviewers.

Is LegMaker commercially available for institutional orders?

LegMaker is an investigational device. Contact Rehabwheel to discuss the current development stage, potential evaluation opportunities and any available purchasing timelines. No delivery date is guaranteed here.

Is the device FDA cleared or approved?

This website does not claim FDA clearance or approval. Request current regulatory status and supporting documentation directly from Rehabwheel before making any clinical or purchasing decision.

Can we obtain a quotation or submit a purchase order inquiry?

Yes. You can submit purchasing requirements for review. An inquiry is not an accepted order, binding quotation or confirmation of product availability.

Open procurement inquiry ↗

What clinical evidence is available?

Review the research page for development and evaluation information. Request current study materials directly; proposed research should not be treated as completed clinical validation.

Review research ↗

What wheelchair models and sizes are compatible?

Compatibility must be confirmed for each intended configuration. The development concept targets manual wheelchair integration, but this page does not guarantee fit with any specific model.

Can our clinical or engineering team request a demonstration?

Yes. Submit a professional inquiry describing your organization, intended evaluation setting and relevant questions. Do not include patient information in the public form.
