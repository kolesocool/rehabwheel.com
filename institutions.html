Institutional review checklist

Use this checklist to organize your internal evaluation. It identifies topics for discussion, not certifications or verified performance claims.

◇
Clinical use and population

Confirm intended setting, patient eligibility, supervision and clinical workflow requirements.

◇
Device compatibility

Review manual wheelchair fit, mounting constraints, setup and facility space needs.

◇
Safety and regulatory pathway

Request current development status, risk controls and applicable regulatory documentation when available.

◇
Evidence and evaluation

Separate completed observations from proposed studies and confirm available evidence.

◇
Service and training

Discuss onboarding, maintenance, technical support and operating procedures.

◇
Procurement readiness

Discuss quotation needs, budget cycles, supplier onboarding and anticipated availability.

Do not submit patient records or protected health information through public inquiry forms.
