Nikita Chizhov

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Nikita Chizhov is an entrepreneur and inventor focused on practical movement technology for people who use wheelchairs. As Rehabwheel’s founder and chief executive officer, he leads the company’s product vision, corporate strategy and development of the LegMaker lower-limb exercise platform.

His work centers on making exercise possible beyond traditional clinical settings through compact mechanical systems designed around the user’s existing wheelchair. At Rehabwheel, his responsibilities connect engineering development with research collaboration, manufacturing planning and conversations with clinical and strategic partners.

CORPORATE OVERVIEW

Medical devices meet physical artificial intelligence.

Rehabwheel Inc. is a pioneering medical device and physical artificial intelligence company dedicated to transforming neurological rehabilitation and mobility independence. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, with research and operational expansions in Austin, Texas, we design and manufacture next-generation assistive mobility hardware that bridges advanced mechatronics with rigorous clinical outcomes.

MISSION & VISION

Engineering for physical autonomy.

MISSION

Intelligent, accessible mobility hardware.

To engineer intelligent, accessible, and clinically validated mobility hardware that restores physical autonomy and redefines neurological rehabilitation standards worldwide.

VISION

Robotic assistance integrated into everyday life.

A future where state-of-the-art robotic assistive systems seamlessly integrate into clinical facilities and daily living environments, empowering individuals with mobility impairments to achieve peak physical independence.

FOCUS

Mechatronics + clinical outcomes.

Rehabwheel’s development direction connects assistive hardware, embedded control, sensing and data-driven rehabilitation workflows.

CORE INNOVATIONS

Patented rehabilitation hardware with an intelligent control roadmap.

Our foundational technology includes the patented LegMaker rehabilitation system, protected by U.S. Patent No. 10,603,541 and international intellectual property. The platform combines precision actuation, custom embedded sensor networks and an edge-AI development roadmap intended to support adaptive, data-driven rehabilitation regimens tailored to individual recovery trajectories.

LegMaker is an investigational device.
