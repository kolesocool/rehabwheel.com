Full description:
Rehabwheel introduces LegMaker, an investigational wheelchair-mounted exercise device designed to explore accessible lower-limb movement for people who use wheelchairs.

Explore product design, view demonstrations and photographs, read about the underlying technology and research efforts, and request additional information or a demonstration from the Rehabwheel team.

App features:
- Discover the LegMaker concept and wheelchair-mounted design.
- Explore the technology, research, and product information.
- View public product photographs and educational material.
- Contact the team or request a demonstration.

Important: LegMaker is an investigational device. Content is informational, not medical advice. Availability, regulatory status, and supported features may vary. This app does not diagnose, treat, or monitor a medical condition.

## Developer checklist
- Confirm developer legal/contact identity and final package ID BEFORE first release.
- Confirm that all screenshots accurately show the actual built Android app.
- Create adaptive launcher icon and a 1024 × 500 feature graphic.
- Complete app access, Data safety, health declarations, ads, content rating, target audience, and privacy-policy fields truthfully after testing. Do not assume a static app collects no data: inspect forms, web views, analytics, and linked pages.
- Do not put patient information, keys, API tokens, or passwords in GitHub.
- Generate private upload signing key and configure signing separately.
- Upload a signed AAB to Internal testing after confirming a successful build and smoke tests.
