Careers at Rehabwheel · San Francisco, CA

Build for a world with more ways to move.

Help us develop and study technology that makes lower-limb exercise more accessible for wheelchair users.

View open roles ↓

How we work

Start with wheelchair users

Learn how people set up, use and store equipment in everyday settings. Bring that feedback into the next build.

Build and test together

Bring engineering, rehabilitation and design into the same conversation, from the first prototype to the next study.

Let evidence guide decisions

Define what we need to learn, document what we observe and use the results to decide what to change.

What we’re building

Exercise from the chair you already use.

LegMaker is an investigational device designed to attach to a manual wheelchair for seated lower-limb exercise. Our work spans wheelchair fit, prototype reliability and the design of early feasibility studies.
