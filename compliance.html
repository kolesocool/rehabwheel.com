REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE

Safety and quality
by design.

Rehabwheel Inc. is committed to meeting applicable global standards for medical device safety, software integrity, risk management, and quality management. Our engineering and operational frameworks are being developed toward rigorous federal and international requirements.

QUALITY MANAGEMENT & MEDICAL STANDARDS

A regulatory framework built alongside the product.

FDA

FDA Compliance Framework

Rehabwheel is developing its regulatory and quality framework with consideration of applicable U.S. Food and Drug Administration requirements for medical devices, including quality-system and manufacturing requirements relevant to the eventual device classification and regulatory pathway.

ISO 13485

Quality Management Systems

Designing toward ISO 13485 principles for medical-device quality management across product development and planned San Francisco and Austin operations.

ISO 14971

Risk Management

Applying a risk-management approach informed by ISO 14971 principles to identify, evaluate, control, and monitor medical-device risks throughout development.

IEC 62304

Software Lifecycle

Developing edge-AI, connected software, and embedded control-system processes with IEC 62304 medical-device software lifecycle principles in mind where applicable.
