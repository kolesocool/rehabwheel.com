PRODUCT QUESTIONS

Understand the product direction.

Does LegMaker replace a wheelchair?+

No. The product concept is designed around a wheelchair setup rather than as a wheelchair replacement.

What does “Passive” mean here?+

In the product concept, Passive describes motor-assisted pedal movement. Final controls and specifications remain subject to development.

What does “Active” mean here?+

Active describes user-driven movement within the intended exercise workflow, with session sensing and tracking included in the product roadmap.

What is planned for Resistance mode?+

The current product direction includes adjustable resistance with up to 15 planned levels.

Are the dashboard and metrics already released?+

No. Interface visuals and connected metrics shown on this site communicate development direction unless explicitly stated otherwise.

PRODUCTION + REGULATORY PATH

Built toward clinical pilots and formal validation.

The current company roadmap calls for an initial 25-unit batch for clinical pilots, usability and safety validation, quality-system work and an anticipated U.S. Class II regulatory pathway. These are plans—not completed clearance or certification.

CURRENT ROADMAP
  • 25-unit initial clinical-pilot batch planned
  • Usability and safety validation preparation
  • Quality-management-system development
  • Class II pathway anticipated; regulatory strategy remains under development
