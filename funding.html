Start with a conversation. LegMaker is under development. Listing a resource does not mean it funds LegMaker, accepts applications now or has partnered with Rehabwheel. Confirm current eligibility and equipment acceptance directly with the provider before applying or spending money.

Different routes. One place to start.

Independent grants

Explore foundation and state-resource contacts. Ask about location, applicant criteria, documentation and permitted equipment.

Loans & financing

These are repayable funding routes, not grants. Review interest, fees, repayment and equipment requirements with the provider.

No. The organizations in this directory operate independently and make their own decisions. This page provides resource contacts.

No. Loans generally require repayment. Confirm all terms with the lender before signing an agreement.
