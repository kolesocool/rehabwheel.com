Funding questions, answered.

Does Rehabwheel award these grants?

No. The organizations in this directory operate independently and make their own decisions. This page provides resource contacts.

Can these resources fund LegMaker?

That has not been confirmed. Each provider must assess the specific product, its availability and your application.

Are loans the same as grants?

No. Loans generally require repayment. Confirm all terms with the lender before signing an agreement.

What should I send Rehabwheel?

Use the contact form for general product or procurement questions. Share medical and financial documents only through a suitable channel agreed with the relevant provider.

Contact each provider for current eligibility and application requirements.
